## Consistent visual rules
- Godot 4 2D; mobile-friendly portrait-first browser game, 720 × 1280 design baseline.
- Cozy, clean pastel chibi cartoon; cream, peach, pink, mint, soft warm wood and chocolate brown.
- Oversized round cat heads, tiny bodies, friendly expressions, cute paws; cats are café employees.
- Woodland animal customers: bunny, fox, bear, raccoon.
- Fixed 2D side-view/dollhouse presentation, not isometric or chase camera.
- Rounded readable UI with large touch targets; avoid clutter and tiny text.
- Treat images as *visual design references*, not as exact game-ready sprites, UI components, or production assets. They contain illustrative text and mockup elements that may be inconsistent.
- Maintain editable native Godot scenes/UI and original separately prepared art assets. Do not stretch a full screenshot into a playable interface.
- Do not introduce new gameplay or alter established mechanics just because a concept sheet depicts an element.

## Implementation handoff

Do not modify project files until explicitly asked to implement changes. Keep Paw Pads Café in its own standalone project/repository, separate from Arcane Survivors.
