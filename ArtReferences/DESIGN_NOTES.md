# Paw Pads Café design reference notes

The user supplied this pack as the official visual direction for the title screen, logo, app icon, and overall branding. The pack's README is reference material, not authorization to implement its depicted gameplay systems.

## Visual direction reviewed

- Reference 01: warm wooden storefront and sign, cream/chocolate rounded lettering, pink paw branding, foliage and hanging lamps. Mochi is an orange tabby with a white muzzle/chest, tiny body, oversized head, and dark barista apron. Play is the strongest pink accent; Settings is a quieter cream button. The icon concept features Mochi holding paw-art coffee against pink.
- Reference 02: peach/cream walls, warm wood counter and furniture, pink seating, mint foliage, brown outlines, rounded cream HUD panels, and illustrated navigation tiles. Its customers and progression indicators are visual context, not additions to the current foundation scope.
- References 03–05 are future-system concept sheets. References 06–07 are early explorations with older branding.

## Implemented foundation refinement

The title uses an editable wooden-sign BrandLogo with cream/pink lettering and a pink paw mark, native storefront framing, a pink primary Play action, and cream Settings. Mochi and the project icon use simple original vector placeholders inspired by the orange-tabby/white-muzzle/barista direction. These are not recreations of the finished illustration.

The café uses warmer wood, peach walls, pink seating, hanging lamps, a paw wall sign, cream HUD statistic cards, and pictorial pastel navigation tiles. All UI remains separate editable Godot controls. Actor placement markers are provided without behavior.

## Remaining art gaps and scope

Finished painted storefront art, the illustrated production logo, and final Mochi sprites remain future asset work. No whole concept sheet is used as a playable interface. Reference 02's customer/progression systems and references 03–05 remain outside foundation scope.

## Validation

Godot 4.6.3 import, launch, title-to-café navigation, modal settings, focus restoration, and headless layout/touch-target checks passed at five sizes. Graphical GL Compatibility screenshots were inspected at portrait, mobile portrait, and desktop landscape sizes; see `Validation/`. Browser export and physical-device testing remain outstanding.
